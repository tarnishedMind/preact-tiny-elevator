ELEVATOR: IDEAS AND BACKLOG
===========================

UI
--
- [ ] Panel for adding a person manually (choose start floor and destination)


GAME MODES
----------
Strategic planning
  Each level has a predefined situation: the elevator load is known and
  visible before the start. The player needs a strategy that clears the level.

  Goal: start the elevator from a given floor and finish in the fewest moves.

  How the player controls the elevator:
  - Customizable elevator algorithm that can be tuned for a specific level
  - Step-by-step manual control of the elevator

Drop off and pick up later
  People have a maximum waiting time that must not be exceeded, so the
  elevator may drop someone off early and pick them up again later.
  Note: this model could be reworked into something that is no longer an
  elevator at all, e.g. extended to a 2D map.


DAY AND NIGHT CYCLE
-------------------
Simulate a working day in an office:
- Morning: people arrive at work
- Night: occasional random rides
